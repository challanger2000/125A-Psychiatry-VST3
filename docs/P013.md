## Why P013 was necessary

The [P012 real-VST3 high-frequency probe](P012.md) found that PSYCHOSIS's 3rd-harmonic alias was improved by its filtered 2× nonlinear core, but **MANIA** and **DEPRESSION** still generated significant folded harmonics at 48 kHz. When a 15-kHz sine's 3rd harmonic is created at the host rate, its 45-kHz component folds to an **unwanted audible 3-kHz signal**. A late ordinary host-rate lowpass cannot remove it once it has folded. Frequency alias numbers are referenced to each effect's output fundamental in dBc.

## Implemented engineering change

- New `src/oversample2x.hpp`: reusable instance-local interpolator/nonlinear callback/decimator, with independent **sixth-order Butterworth 2nd-order sections** on the up and down paths and a nominal 19.68-kHz cutoff at host 48 kHz. Per-channel fixed-size filter state, coefficients prepared outside processing. Processes two sub-samples for every original sample, with no heap allocations, mutexes or threads.
- `MANIA`, in `src/three_worlds.cpp` and `.hpp`: the `tanh` saturation and short recurrence/feedback now operate on the filtered upsampled excitation at 2×. The ring/tremolo modulation and envelope-responsive speed remain original host-rate elements. This changes the timbre of the actual processed signal; it does **not** claim identical phase/time response to P012.
- `DEPRESSION`, in `src/depression.cpp` and `.hpp`: its level-driven `tanh` saturator now processes interpolated 2× audio and returns a separately lowpass-decimated signal; dark filtering and delay network remain in the existing host-rate processing order. A subsequent reverb-return saturator remains unchanged, so the entire effect is not yet proven alias-free.
- Macro **0% = bit-identical Dry**, **100% = full internally processed Wet with no additional original Dry branch**, and global inverse THERAPY **0%=Wet / 100%=Dry**. The serial macro order, stable VST3 class and parameter IDs, component-state v4 and UI-state v2 do not change.
- P013 introduces a mandatory **−50 dBc alias ceiling** for MANIA and DEPRESSION on both defined 14 and 15-kHz input test tones; P012 PSYCHOSIS −45 dBc ceiling remains in place.

## Verified quantitative results from real `Processor::process`

Final [Windows VST3 and actual-audio test run 45285249372](https://github.com/challanger2000/125A-Psychiatry-VST3/actions/runs/45285249372), **SUCCESS**, source `37988d988b8180eb28c99d5f53d51e4d0ff67e2a`:

| Real-VST3 effect | 14 kHz → folded 6 kHz, before | 14 kHz → 6 kHz, after | 15 kHz → folded 3 kHz, before | 15 kHz → 3 kHz, after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **MANIA** | −21.41 dBc | **−61.72 dBc** | −20.22 dBc | **−57.32 dBc** |
| **DEPRESSION** | −26.57 dBc | **−73.31 dBc** | −20.47 dBc | **−59.03 dBc** |
| PSYCHOSIS P012 control | −65.25 dBc | **−65.25 dBc** | −59.47 dBc | **−59.47 dBc** |
| PANIC untouched control | −31.86 dBc | **−31.86 dBc** | −31.87 dBc | **−31.87 dBc** |

Suppression at 15 kHz relative to P012: **MANIA +37.10 dB; DEPRESSION +38.56 dB**. Dry reference contains essentially no energy at these folded frequencies. All spectral measurements use a **48-kHz host rate** and a 60,000-sample stereo tone, ignoring the first 12,000 samples for warm-up. This is a *single-tone third-harmonic* metric; other harmonics, intermodulation and other musical materials are not automatically cleared.
