## Change

P013 left PANIC single-tone third-harmonic aliasing at −31.87 dBc (48 kHz, 15-kHz sine, fold at 3 kHz). P014 moved the wet-only PANIC excitation and tanh shaper into the same prepared allocation-free 2× Butterworth interpolation/decimation utility. The trigger, envelope, transient and tremor mechanisms remain otherwise unchanged. There is no added Dry in the 100%-Wet processing path.

| Real Windows VST3, THERAPY=0%, PANIC 100% | P013 | P014 | Improvement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 14-kHz tone / 6-kHz folded alias | −31.86 dBc | **−73.41 dBc** | **41.55 dB** |
| 15-kHz tone / 3-kHz folded alias | −31.87 dBc | **−69.35 dBc** | **37.48 dB** |

These are *specific third-harmonic single-tone* measurements. The P014 real-VST3 test now rejects PANIC aliases above −45 dBc at both measured conditions. Other macro tests remain: MANIA/DEPRESSION ≤−50 dBc, PSYCHOSIS ≤−45 dBc.
