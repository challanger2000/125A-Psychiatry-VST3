## Method

The real `psychiatry::vst3::Processor::process` audio test creates a deterministic stereo two-tone input at 14,000 + 15,000 Hz (0.175 peak amplitude per tone; worst-case combined absolute amplitude 0.35) at 44,100 / 48,000 / 96,000 Hz. After 12,000 input samples warm-up, each tone and third-order product is measured over exactly one second at the actual host rate. The original Dry signal is confirmed free of the tested aliases.

Every one of the nine macro processors is tested at each host rate for **0% exact original Dry**, **100% THERAPY exact Dry**, finite 100%-Wet samples and an absolute sample-peak guard of <4.0. For four nonlinear worlds (DEPRESSION/MANIA/PANIC/PSYCHOSIS), four products are measured per rate: 3×14 kHz = 42 kHz, 3×15 kHz = 45 kHz, 2×14+15 = 43 kHz and 2×15+14 = 44 kHz. At 44.1 and 48 kHz the products **fold into lower frequencies**; at 96 kHz they are *in-band* (below its 48-kHz Nyquist frequency), **not folded aliases**.

**Meter definition:** this test reports `20 log10(product amplitude / combined Dry-input 14/15-kHz amplitude)`, in dB relative to the input tone pair. It is **not** effect-output-carrier dBc. The earlier P012–P014 single-tone aliases remain separately guarded using output-carrier dBc.

## Important remaining limits

The test is specific to **four** nonlinear macro worlds and **four** product lines per rate, not an exhaustive multi-tone spectral scan. Five other worlds may produce other artifacts. Wet-only sixth-order IIR filters introduce group delay/phase effects at partial THERAPY mixtures; transients and dry/wet combing need verification. Musical recordings, long-session CPU/no-denormal stress, end-user Studio Pro project recall and final historically themed licensed GUI still need acceptance. No early end-user test is requested.
