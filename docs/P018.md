Local FIR proof: 49-tap symmetric polyphase halfband, 24 host samples per complete 2x stage. GCC C++17 probe: exact impulse symmetry, DC .999999996, 18 compensated phase points at 44.1/48/96k and 100–16000Hz with magnitude deviation <.002 dB and negligible phase; four tanh folded probes −95.12 to −105.22 dBc. Not whole-plugin results.

Factory source **0.9.7** integrates the new oversampler in DEPRESSION, MANIA, PANIC, PSYCHOSIS; each stage has a matched 24-sample raw dry delay and matching delayed mix envelope, including its exact neutral state. DEPRESSION's parallel body is aligned. Total fixed latency 96 samples, exposed to the VST3 host via getLatencySamples. Global THERAPY and native bypass are matched to that delay. Pending parameter events are bounded per-lane and delayed by the earlier stage latencies, with no heap in the audio path. Bypass keeps wet history running so toggling does not restart the chain. Exact dry after PDC is mandatory. Standalone references, real-processor padded/cropped renders, automation and impulse tests distinguish raw causality from compensated FIR pre-ringing. Prior alias thresholds retained.
