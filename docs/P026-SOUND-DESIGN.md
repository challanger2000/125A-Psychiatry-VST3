## Recipes and routing
These are concrete starting points, not already implemented or measured values. Macro positions coordinate component parameters and branch balances. Arrows are serial; '+' denotes parallel branches. Ranges below are initial tuning hypotheses. At zero exact latency-compensated dry; at 100% deliberately transformed, no accidental dry blend. Original-derived excitation within the wet recipe is permitted. Existing THERAPY inversion retained. No new controls per world.

| World | Signal architecture | Evolution and rejection condition |
|---|---|---|
| DEPRESSION | Transient attenuation → slow leveling compression → resonant dark lowpass (roughly 6 kHz down to 250–600 Hz) → subtle downward drifting resampler; parallel send from processed body into dark diffuse FDN → damping → decaying granular fragmentation of return. | Body loses attack and presence before room grows; high intensity becomes oppressive, unstable decay, not clean reverb with original signal dominating. Keep current FDN core if suitable. |
| PARANOIA | Input/lookback → irregular event-selected reverse fragments (40–300 ms) + asymmetrical multitap echoes (30–400 ms) → moving comb filter in bounded feedback → unpredictable stereo pan; branches sum with filtered forward body. | Uneasy spatial pursuit, unexpected fragments, not rhythmic fixed repeat or ordinary ping pong. Retain useful current reverse and feedback engine. |
| SCHIZOPHRENIA | Split into independently evolving pitch/formant-shifted voices (two initially, up to three/four; pitch ranges e.g. -7 to +7 semitones, separate phase/readheads) → distinct formant coloration and independent pan → limited pairwise ring/cross modulation → sum. Align initial attacks as far as practical; avoid audible fixed slapback lag. | Recognizably divergent timbres simultaneously, conflicting intervals, spectral collision; reject if just doubled delayed original. Current shared readhead phase and 114 ms base lag require replacement/improvement. |
| MANIA | Input envelope → gradually accelerating capture/retrigger (e.g. 2–24 Hz) → chopper/AM with rising density → resonant moving bandpass → bounded feedback saturation. | Persistent escalating pulse and densification; no large random holes or isolated panic events. Preserve good existing chopper, add actual retrigger rather than calling AM retrigger. |
| PANIC | Transient detector plus bounded level-gated irregular event scheduler → short envelope-shaped duck/dropouts (e.g. 15–90 ms) → rapid falling/jerking pitch-readhead gestures on live input → transient-keyed bandpassed input/noise crackle and occasional short backwards fragment → soft bounded sum. | Isolated jolts, burst clusters, surprise and recovery. Remove independent sinusoidal siren and continuous chirp. No steady tremolo (MANIA), no continuous missing bands (AMNESIA), no damage spikes; silence must not trigger. |
| DISSOCIATION | Dual-buffer overlapping granular processor → independent forward/reverse grain schedules (40–200 ms) → time dispersion and stereo decorrelation → diffused spatial branch. | Liquid detached continuity with flowing grains, not abrupt gaps, stable static freeze or endless repetitions. Preserve current frozen reverse capture where useful, evolve into overlap/add. |
| OBSESSION | Detect/capture a complete microphrase → immutable held loop → repeat counts and staircase loop shortening (e.g. 300 to 15 ms) → progressive feedback filtering, saturation and repetition-dependent timbral deformation (quantization only if a uniquely convincing benefit is demonstrated) → bounded release/new capture. | Recognizable same fragment compulsively repeated; rhythmic rather than stochastic gaps. Preserve verified immutable capture, fades and endpoints; assess musical response before altering. |
| HALLUCINATION | Analytic frequency shifter → parallel formant filters and narrow resonator bank with detuned resonances → cross-modulation by shifted input → reverse/diffuse tail. | Source-derived phantom harmonics/voice-like overtones floating around source; no independent sampled voice or siren. Retain verified shifter; add genuinely resonant and formant-shaped parallel paths as needed. |
| PSYCHOSIS | Phase-aligned three-band crossover → strongly driven asymmetric hard/soft clipping (oversampled) → full-range midband wavefolder (multiple folds, not weak sine blend) + inter-band ring/product branch → resonant filtering inside the distortion stages and strongly driven upper-band clipping → recombine with controlled low foundation → calibrated peak safety. | A seriously aggressive, punchy, dirty EBM/Industrial distortion, from usable driven synth bass to extreme nonlinear deformation; retain low-end pressure without an intact dry overlay. NO bitcrushing or sample-rate reduction in PSYCHOSIS. Drive normalized relative to input with bounded slow envelope compensation; avoid automatic gain flattening the texture. No chirps, reverse or reverb as dominant features. Oversample nonlinear stages as needed; no bitcrushing, sample-rate reduction or gratuitous digital artifacts. |
| DELIRIUM | Continuous variable-speed resampling from bounded history → random smooth speed trajectories (roughly 0.4–1.6x) → occasional discontinuity-safe jump/readhead crossfade → tape-stop style drift and recovery. | Whole sound lurches and loses tempo/pitch reference; not simultaneous harmonized copies (SCHIZOPHRENIA), not random chopped holes (AMNESIA), not regular rising repetition (MANIA). Causal only, no lookahead promise; handle finite buffer boundaries. |
| CATATONIA | Spectral analysis → capture one stable complex spectral frame → phase-propagating overlap/add freeze → slowly narrow/decay spectral content → hold/release envelope with rare brief input re-entry. | Sound stands still for long intervals, can abruptly thaw; not a looping temporal waveform. FFT latency and silence/reset semantics measured, no infinite self-sustaining output unless explicitly designed and correctly reported. |
| AMNESIA | Deterministic stochastic mask → whole-signal time erasure plus independent frequency-band erasure with phase-aligned crossover → sporadic bounded resurfacing of incomplete captured fragment → short ramps/crossfades. | Missing information, gaps, incomplete recovery. Unlike PANIC, no added shock or aggressive burst; unlike OBSESSION, no same fragment repeating. At maximum substantial missing content is intended, not complete permanent silence. |

## Differentiation and architecture checks
- MANIA = accelerating regularity; PANIC = transient shock and recovery; OBSESSION = recognizable repeated fragment; AMNESIA = missing information.
- SCHIZOPHRENIA = multiple simultaneous timbres; HALLUCINATION = added input-derived phantom spectral components; PSYCHOSIS = nonlinear destruction of original timbre.
- DEPRESSION = energy loss/decay; DISSOCIATION = fluid time dispersion; DELIRIUM = unstable whole-signal speed; CATATONIA = arrested spectral time; PARANOIA = displaced echoes.
- Each stage must audibly contribute when intended; do not add algorithms only to make lists impressive. Test stage isolation/bypass and combination before committing topology. Remove redundant stages that do not help. Preserve successful sounds; PANIC and PSYCHOSIS first.
- No blanket loudness matching inside the effect that suppresses its creative dynamics; use raw and RMS-matched comparisons for assessment. At higher positions (50–100%) the character can be dramatic; lower 0–50% need not be forced more extreme.
- Existing nine parameter IDs 100–108, THERAPY 200, native bypass 999, existing class IDs remain stable. Reserve 109–111 for new macros; append versioned state, restore older v1–v4 data with new amounts zero. Update randomizer, recall, automation, monitor model and GUI wiring for 12 consistently later; do not reuse old IDs or silently reinterpret old state. New DSP can change old active macro sounds; document that sound recall is not necessarily bit-identical across revised versions.
- Keep PDC fixed per processing setup, compensate all branches and neutral/THERAPY/bypass for added FFT or pitch latency. Avoid speculative FFT size/latency claims; select and measure. No callback allocation/locking, finite-state deterministic restart, smooth transitions, mono/stereo, multirate, bounded feedback. New tail reporting must account for freeze.
- First deliver revised PANIC/PSYCHOSIS against old reference, then review remaining seven and implement new three. Compare isolated 50/75/100% and mixed macros on synth/bass, drums, guitar, voice and atmosphere; include tails and true zero input. Use actual audio recordings, exported A/B and measurable evidence; spectral/level metrics do not prove sound quality. Final Studio Pro listening only after engineering. GUI art and MOTION postponed; current placeholders rejected.

## Confirmed user decisions — 2026-11-24 15:17 (conversation)

- **Sound first.** Do not proceed straight from an evocative name to implementation or from a green validator to a claim of convincing sound. Before coding, define the perceptual target, concrete individual FX, serial/parallel/feedback topology, interaction, macro trajectory and differentiation; critically evaluate whether the combination plausibly achieves that target. Keep only algorithms that contribute to the audible goal. This applies to subsequent chats and future 125A projects as a working principle.
- **PSYCHOSIS:** a genuinely brutal, fat, gritty, forceful EBM/Industrial distortion, particularly useful on synth bass. Asymmetric distortion, strong hard clipping, genuine multi-fold waveshaping, multiband distortion, intermodulation and resonant filtering within the drive path. Preserve low-end pressure and control output level without making the distortion polite. **No bitcrushing and no sample-rate reduction in PSYCHOSIS.** User especially dislikes bitcrushing generally; do not use it by default anywhere, including OBSESSION. Only consider it elsewhere if a convincing unique musical benefit is established. No gratuitous bit degradation.
- **GUI target (only after audio):** exactly 12 equal-size effect knobs distributed in a ring, like clock positions, around a large circular central image window/bull's-eye. Separate THERAPY knob and LOSS OF CONTROL randomization button; randomize all twelve effect macros only. Small MOTION and DE/EN switches. Keep readable horizontal labels, usable spacing. No additional unnecessary global controls. Clockwise starting at 12 o'clock: DEPRESSION, PARANOIA, SCHIZOPHRENIA, MANIA, PANIC, DISSOCIATION, OBSESSION, HALLUCINATION, PSYCHOSIS, DELIRIUM, CATATONIA, AMNESIA. This ordering is a design starting point, not a requirement to change stable parameter IDs.
- **Visual atmosphere:** cold, oppressive, clinical, desaturated steel/blue-gray/anthracite, pale icy or gray-blue light. Aged matte metal, heavy glass and mechanical bezel; NOT warm orange/amber, boutique amp styling, or colorful neon. Replace rejected procedural placeholder images with convincing realistic historical-style monochrome room/equipment imagery; no patient exploitation. MOTION must affect the whole visual field in distinct macro-dependent ways (ghosting, rolls, double exposure, analog interference, etc.), not only a tiny displaced strip. Static when off. Respect rights and avoid rapid flashes.
- **Preserve the user's latest audition decisions:** several macros become convincing above 50%, and that is fine. Do not globally steepen curves. PANIC and PSYCHOSIS are highest priority, followed by review of remaining seven; then add three distinct worlds. Retain working parts and compare against v0.9.12. No GUI work until audio direction is settled. Final Studio Pro acceptance after engineering, no needless intermediate user tests.

## Owner-approved optional acoustic sound layers (2026-11-24)

Selected macros may blend in a matching atonal acoustic layer (whispers, breathing, footsteps, creaks, machinery, metal) alongside their compound FX. Macro governs both; no extra controls, no forced layer on every macro. Artistic effect, not a portrayal of real patients. Signal-dependent triggering, envelope-controlled amount, varied source fragments, stereo movement and per-world processing; no gratuitous looping or autonomous noise on silence. Preserve exact neutral and inverse THERAPY. PSYCHOSIS remains distortion-first. See [source library and provenance](../sound-library/README.md). Audition and verify decoding, licenses and levels before shipping. Raw assets are not integrated yet.
